# U8 market risk: VaR and ES estimators, scoring and comparison, FRTB desk tests and the market back-tests (U8-E)

Sources: research dossier 05 of the Contraste initiative (market risk, sections A.0 to A.2 for the estimators and B.1 to B.6 for
the back-tests and the comparison, and "Implications for Contraste" for the ladder and the leakage-safe windows) and dossier 01
(Basel pillar 1, section D.4: MAR32.9 Table 1, the desk limits of MAR32.18 to MAR32.19 and the PLA test of MAR32.36 to
MAR32.42), and the market API contract `engine-market-api` 1.0.0 (Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/market-api.md`, reviewed by
Opus on 2026-10-13), whose functions, units, invariants and golden values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into
`docs/market/`. Where the contract marks a detail UNVERIFIED, the primary source read for this unit is named on the docs page
and the row below states what was implemented.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The engine
computes and states no compliance claim: the alphas of a light, the thresholds and the replication counts are policy parameters
the caller supplies or Contraste internal gates. The estimators work in the return convention (VaR and ES negative); positive
loss numbers exist only at the reporting layer, through `conventions.to_loss_numbers`.
