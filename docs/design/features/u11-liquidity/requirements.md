# U11 Liquidity: run-off, counterbalancing capacity, ladders, scenarios, reverse stress, the depositor generator, the LCR and NSFR calculators and the validation tests (U11-E)

Sources: research dossier 05 of the Contraste initiative (market, CCR, ALM, liquidity and operational risk; section E.1 to E.5 for the
ladder, the counterbalancing capacity, the run-off evidence and the reverse stress), dossier 01 (Basel pillar 1 and 2, section G.1 to
G.3: LCR20, LCR30, LCR40, LCR99, NSF20, NSF30, NSF99, SRP50), dossier 08 (G.2 and G.4: the generator with known truth), the U11 research
addendum (L1 the ECB LiST 2019 tables, L4 the Federal Reserve margins) and the engine contract `engine-liquidity-api` 1.0.0 (Contraste
`docs/contracts/engine/liquidity-api.md`, the version after the Opus review's fix round, commit a3844ff), whose functions, units,
invariants and golden values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/liquidity/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The engine computes and states no compliance claim: the LCR is a Basel formula applied to aggregates with stated assumptions and is never
the bank's reported or regulatory LCR. Rates are decimals per relevant day, cumulative outflow is 1 - (1 - q)^n, a survival period is
"survives the horizon" with the horizon when the net liquidity position never turns negative, never a number. A missing input is refused
with the field named, never filled. The golden values are reproduced by tests that recompute them through a second, independent route
(a loop, a closed form or the printed table of the source), never by a test that only types the answer.
