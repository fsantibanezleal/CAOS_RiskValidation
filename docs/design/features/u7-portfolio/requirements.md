# U7 Portfolio credit capital: copula Monte Carlo, CreditRisk+, granularity, importance sampling, Euler, the Vasicek fit and the economic capital tests (U7-E)

Sources: research dossier 04 of the Contraste initiative (credit risk models, sections C.4 for the Vasicek and ASRF model and
H.1 to H.8 for portfolio credit risk, and the ladder "Portfolio credit risk", rungs C1 to C6), dossier 05 (G.4, the Euler
principle, and G.5, the validation tests of an economic capital model), and the portfolio API contract `engine-portfolio-api`
1.0.0 (Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/portfolio-api.md`, reviewed by Opus on 2026-10-13), whose functions, units,
invariants and golden values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/portfolio/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The engine
computes and states no compliance claim: confidence levels, tolerances, light parameters and seeds are parameters the caller
supplies or Contraste internal gates. A value that cannot be computed is a `ValueError` or a `not_evaluated` result, never a
placeholder number. The existing `engines.vasicek` and `regulatory.irb` are the analytic anchors and are reused, never
duplicated.
