# U5 provisions: the IFRS 9 expected credit loss engine and its validation battery (U5-E1)

Sources: research dossier 02 of the Contraste initiative (IFRS 9 expected credit loss and provisioning, read on
2026-10-04, sections A, B, C.1 to C.3, I.1, I.2 and I.5) and the provisions API contract `engine-provisions-api` 1.0.0
(Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/provisions-api.md`, reviewed and fixed on 2026-10-12), whose functions, units, invariants
and golden values this unit implements one by one. The local-regulation modules `provisions/local_cl.py` and
`provisions/local_es.py` belong to U5-E2 and are not specified here. Transcribed into `docs/provisions/ecl/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The engine computes and states no compliance claim: multipliers, floors, probation lengths and horizons are policy
parameters the caller supplies, and only the 30 and 90 days past due presumptions are in IFRS 9.
