# U3 known-truth generators and the size and power harness

Sources: research dossier 12 of the Contraste initiative (read from the primary sources on 2026-10-05: Morris, White
and Crowther 2019 for the design of simulation studies and the Monte Carlo standard error; BCBS Working Paper 14,
pages 41 to 67, for the binomial test under correlation and the published simulation of the normal and traffic-lights
tests; Yurdakul and Naranjo 2020, Table 4, for the PSI; Demler, Pencina and D'Agostino 2012 for DeLong on nested models;
Brown, Cai and DasGupta 2001 for the Wilson interval), dossier 08 section G (the generators and the truth labels) and
dossier 07 (a generator ships with a verifier and its configuration). Transcribed into `docs/generators/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| RV-301 | EVERY generator SHALL be a frozen configuration that serialises itself to JSON-safe values, draws one dataset from a NumPy `Generator`, returns its truth computed exactly (closed form or deterministic quadrature), gives the same dataset for the same seed, and refuses parameters outside their domain. | `tests/test_generators.py::test_generator_contract` |
| RV-302 | THE default-count generator SHALL implement the model of BCBS WP14's simulation study (factor correlation $\vartheta^{\lvert s-t\rvert}$ across years, asset correlation $\rho_t$ within year $t$, conditional PD $\Phi((\Phi^{-1}(p_t)-\sqrt{\rho_t}S_t)/\sqrt{1-\rho_t})$, binomial counts given the factor); its verifier SHALL reproduce on its own stream the true PDs, the exact variance of every count and the factor correlations within four Monte Carlo standard errors; and the default correlation it reports SHALL reproduce the columns of WP14 Examples 1 to 5 to the printed two decimals. | `tests/test_generators.py::test_default_counts_follow_wp14_model`, `tests/test_generators.py::test_default_correlation_wp14_examples` |
| RV-303 | THE binormal score generator SHALL give each score's AUC as $\Phi(\delta/\sqrt 2)$ and the exact variance of the Mann-Whitney AUC; THE logistic portfolio SHALL give the true PD of every obligor, the PD of a model with a planted calibration intercept and slope, and its truths by deterministic quadrature; each verifier SHALL reproduce its truths within four Monte Carlo standard errors. | `tests/test_generators.py::test_binormal_truth`, `tests/test_generators.py::test_logistic_portfolio_truth` |
| RV-304 | THE two-sample generator SHALL implement Yurdakul and Naranjo's design (base $N(0, \sigma^2)$, target shifted and scaled, ten bins at the base population's deciles or at the base sample's) and give the exact bin probabilities of both populations. | `tests/test_generators.py::test_two_sample_truth` |
| RV-305 | THE migration generator SHALL draw multinomial rows from a true matrix, provide a matrix that decreases away from the diagonal and the planted cell that breaks it, and THE grade-frequency generator SHALL draw multinomial grade counts with the planted concentration; both SHALL give their exact expected counts and, for frequencies, the population CV and Herfindahl index. | `tests/test_generators.py::test_rating_generators_truth` |
| RV-306 | THE harness SHALL spawn one independent stream per repetition from one seed, so a single repetition re-runs alone to the same result; SHALL pass a fresh repetition seed to a test with its own randomness; SHALL count a result without a p-value as undefined and not rejected; and SHALL report, for every level and decision rule, the rejections, the rate, its Monte Carlo standard error $\sqrt{r(1-r)/n}$ (Morris et al. 2019, Table 6) and the 95% Wilson interval. | `tests/test_harness.py::test_streams_rates_and_intervals` |
| RV-307 | FOR a test that depends on the data through one default count (`pd.binomial`, `pd.binomial_vasicek`, `pd.jeffreys`), THE exact rejection probability (the critical count found by evaluating the registered test, the tail of the one-factor mixture) SHALL agree with the harness's simulated rate within 3.29 Monte Carlo standard errors in every checked cell, and SHALL give the true size of the independence-based test of WP14 Example 3 under asset correlation. | `tests/test_harness.py::test_exact_rejection_matches_simulation` |
| RV-308 | THE normal and traffic-lights tests on the default-count generator with the scenarios of WP14 Tables 5 and 6 SHALL reproduce the type I errors of Table 7 and the type II errors of Table 8 within 3.29 combined standard errors $\sqrt{SE^2 + SE^2_{WP14}}$ (25,000 runs each; the traffic lights with the upper tie rule), every cell outside it listed with the reason: 143 of the 144 cells agree. | `tests/test_reproductions.py::test_wp14_tables_7_and_8` |
| RV-309 | THE PSI with the rule-of-thumb bands and with the chi-square benchmark, on the two-sample generator in Yurdakul and Naranjo's design, SHALL reproduce their Table 4 within 3.29 combined standard errors (10,000 runs each), every cell outside it listed with the reason: 53 of the 54 cells agree with the suite's seed, the mean z of the difference staying below 1.2 (the harness sits slightly above the paper, by about 0.7 SE on average, a systematic offset the docs state). | `tests/test_reproductions.py::test_yurdakul_naranjo_table4` |
| RV-310 | THE DeLong test SHALL hold its size within Monte Carlo error on two non-nested scores of equal AUC scored on an independent sample, and THE harness SHALL reproduce Demler et al.'s nested misuse (linear discriminant models fitted and compared on the same Framingham-like sample, 1,000 repetitions) with a rejection rate at 5% below 1%. | `tests/test_reproductions.py::test_delong_nested_and_non_nested` |
| RV-311 | EVERY registered test with a p-value SHALL be run by the suite on a generator where its null holds at the boundary with its assumptions met, and SHALL hold its size at 5% within $\alpha + 3.09\sqrt{\alpha(1-\alpha)/n}$, or its documentation page and result notes SHALL state the measured size. | `tests/test_size.py::test_size_of_every_p_value_test` |
| RV-312 | THE Hosmer-Lemeshow test SHALL use as many degrees of freedom as groups for PDs given before the outcomes, and $G-2$ only when the caller states that the PDs were fitted on the same observations; the measured size on an independent sample SHALL lie within the bound with $G$ and above it with $G-2$, the evidence for the default being kept in the suite. | `tests/test_size.py::test_hosmer_lemeshow_degrees_of_freedom_by_measurement` |
| RV-313 | THE traffic-lights test SHALL compute its p-value from a cached table of the colour profiles' cumulative probabilities and return the same p-value as the full enumeration for every profile with up to nine years. | `tests/test_pd_calibration.py::test_traffic_lights_table_equals_enumeration` |
| RV-314 | THE harness SHALL measure an estimator against its truth with the performance measures of Morris et al. (2019, Table 6): bias, empirical standard error, average model standard error and interval coverage, each with its Monte Carlo standard error. | `tests/test_harness.py::test_estimator_performance` |
| RV-315 | THE generators and the harness SHALL depend only on NumPy and SciPy, so the same wheel runs in Pyodide. | `tests/test_imports.py::test_core_dependencies` |
| RV-316 | THE runnable example of the unit SHALL exercise every generator, the harness and the exact rejection probabilities, and run in the test suite. | `tests/test_examples.py::test_size_power_example_runs` |
| RV-317 | WHERE the development AUC of `disc.auc_vs_initial` is itself an estimate on an independent sample, THE test SHALL accept its variance and divide by the standard error of the difference, saying so in the result; measured on a development sample as large as the current one, the ECB form SHALL exceed the size bound and the extended form hold it. | `tests/test_size.py::test_auc_vs_initial_with_an_estimated_development_auc`, `tests/test_discrimination.py::test_auc_vs_initial_with_the_development_variance` |
| RV-318 | WHERE the environment variable `RISKVALIDATION_SIZE_WORKERS` is above 1, THE release gate's `sizepower` suites SHALL run each registered size study through `riskvalidation.generators.size_sharded.run_sharded` with the study's own seed and repetitions, and the rejection counts, undefined counts and element-wise p-values, metrics and statistics SHALL equal the unsharded run; WHERE it is unset or 1 the suites SHALL run as before, and a value that is zero, negative, fractional or not an integer SHALL raise an error naming the variable. | `tests/test_size_study_sharded.py::test_ml_shift_resilience_gate_path_sharded_equals_unsharded_element_by_element`, `tests/test_size_study_sharded.py::test_the_workers_variable_refuses_zero_and_junk_naming_itself`, `tests/test_size_study_sharded.py::test_simulate_registered_above_one_worker_shards_and_equals_the_unsharded_run`, `tests/test_size_study_sharded.py::test_sharded_counts_equal_simulate_exactly` |
