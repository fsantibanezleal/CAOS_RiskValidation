# U9 CCR: Hull-White one-factor exposure, swaps, exposure measures, collateral and MPoR, initial margin, CVA and wrong-way risk, and the CCR validation tests (U9-E2)

Sources: research dossier 05 of the Contraste initiative (section C, counterparty credit risk and CVA, and the counterparty ladder at line 476), the U9
research addendum (R1 wrong-way risk, R2 dynamic initial margin, R3 the CSA and the margin period of risk, R4 the Hull-White closed forms read from the
QuantLib 1.44 source, R5 the PIT backtest, R6 the AR(1) calibration, R9 the CRE53 definitions) and the CCR API contract `engine-ccr-api` 1.0.0 (Contraste
`docs/contracts/engine/ccr-api.md`, reviewed by Opus on 2026-10-18, fix round of 2026-10-19), whose functions, units, invariants and golden values this unit
implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/ccr/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The engine computes and states no compliance claim. EE is the positive exposure to the counterparty, ENE the bank's own reported positive, CVA a
positive cost and DVA a positive benefit. Rates are decimals compounded continuously, times are years, the margin period of risk is in business days
with 250 to the year. ORE and QuantLib never enter the engine: the reconciliation against them is the C12 bake's, and these tests check the closed
forms against Monte Carlo and against identities. The golden values are reproduced by tests that recompute them through a second route, never by a
test that only types the answer. Monte Carlo is seeded. A missing or invalid input is refused with the field named.
