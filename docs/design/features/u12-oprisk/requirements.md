# U12 Operational risk: the LDA laws and fits, the aggregate loss by Monte Carlo, Panjer and FFT, the SLA, scenarios, the OPE25 standardised approach and the validation tests (U12-E)

Sources: research dossier 05 of the Contraste initiative (section F: F.1 to F.6, and the operational risk ladder and case O1),
dossier 01 (section F: OPE25), Shevchenko (2010), "Implementing loss distribution approach for operational risk"
(arXiv 1008.1108, J. Operational Risk 5(2):3-40; Tables 1 and 2, Example 9.4, equations 36 and 70), and the operational risk
API contract `engine-oprisk-api` 1.0.0 (Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/oprisk-api.md`, reviewed by Opus on 2026-10-13), whose
functions, units, invariants and golden values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/oprisk/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The engine computes and states no compliance claim: the LDA is the economic-capital and validation tool of dossier 05 F.1, never
a Pillar 1 model; collection thresholds, quantile levels and light parameters are policy parameters the caller supplies. A tail
index of 1 or more has no mean: no function returns a finite mean-based number for it.
