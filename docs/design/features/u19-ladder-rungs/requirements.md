# U19 Ladder rungs: Clayton sampling, vine copulas, the Minnesota BVAR and the panel quantile satellite (BL-078)

Sources: the research report of the Contraste initiative on these four rungs (CAOS_MANAGE
`wip/contraste/m14-ladder-rungs-research-2026-11-15.md`: D1 Clayton, D2 vines, D3 the Minnesota prior, D4 the panel quantile
estimators, D5 the interfaces and known-answer tests), the report on the two critiques of the two-step panel quantile estimator
(`wip/contraste/m14-panel-quantile-critiques-2026-11-15.md`), and the decisions of 2026-11-15 (CAOS_MANAGE
`plans/contraste/post-1-0-cycle.md`, "Engine 1.3.0"). Primary texts: Hofert (2008) Algorithm 1 and McNeil and Neslehova (2009)
Example 2.3 (Clayton); Aas, Czado, Frigessi and Bakken (2009) and Dissmann, Brechmann, Czado and Kurowicka (2013) (vines);
Banbura, Giannone and Reichlin (2008) equations (2), (5) to (9) and Karlsson (2012) equations (13) to (22) (Minnesota prior);
Koenker (2004) equations (2.2) to (2.4), Canay (2011) equations (4.1) and (4.2) and Table 1, Besstremyannaya and Golovan (2019),
Chen and Huo (2021) (panel quantile). Transcribed into `docs/aggregation/07_clayton-and-vines.md` and
`docs/stress/07_bvar-and-panel-quantile.md`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The golden
values are recomputed by the tests through a second, independent route (a closed form, an identity, a published table or another
package), never typed as the answer. Randomness is `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(seed))`, as in the rest of the engine. A call that
does not use a new name returns what 1.2.0 returned (`docs/api-policy.md`: names are added, none changed).

## Clayton sampling (`aggregation/copulas.py`)

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| RV-1901 | THE `clayton_kendall_tau` SHALL be theta / (theta + 2) (1/3, 1/2 and 2/3 at theta 1, 2 and 4) and `clayton_theta_from_tau` its inverse 2 tau / (1 - tau), each refusing a parameter outside its open domain (theta > 0, tau in (0, 1)) naming it. | `tests/aggregation/test_clayton_sampling.py::test_kendall_tau_and_inverse` |
| RV-1902 | THE `simulate_clayton_uniforms` SHALL draw the d-dimensional Clayton copula by the Marshall-Olkin frailty algorithm (V ~ Gamma(1/theta, 1), U = (1 - ln X / V)^(-1/theta)) for any d of 2 or more and by the conditional inverse of the h-function for d = 2, keep every draw inside (UNIFORM_FLOOR, 1 - UNIFORM_FLOOR), return the survival copula 1 - U when `survival=True` (the default, so the dependence sits in the joint large-loss tail), be reproducible for a seed and chunked as `simulate_component_losses`, and refuse theta of 0 or less, d below 2, n below 1 and the conditional method at d above 2. | `tests/aggregation/test_clayton_sampling.py::test_simulate_shape_seed_survival_and_refusals` |
| RV-1903 | THE Clayton draws SHALL have the empirical Kendall tau of 400,000 pairs within 0.003 of theta / (theta + 2) at theta 1, 2 and 4, the empirical C(0.5, 0.5) within 0.003 of (2 x 0.5^-theta - 1)^(-1/theta) and the trivariate C(0.5, 0.5, 0.5) at theta 2 within 0.003 of 0.31623, and the two algorithms at d = 2 SHALL agree in distribution (a two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test on C(U1, U2) not rejecting at 1%). | `tests/aggregation/test_clayton_sampling.py::test_draws_match_tau_and_the_copula`, `tests/aggregation/test_clayton_sampling.py::test_two_algorithms_agree_at_d2` |
| RV-1904 | THE h-function of the Clayton copula and its inverse SHALL invert each other on a fixed grid of (u1, u2) in (0.01, 0.99) and theta in {0.5, 1, 2, 4}: to 1e-12 in the conditional probability w (h(h^-1(w | u2) | u2) = w), and in u1 to 1e-12 plus the rounding of w propagated through the copula density (a bound of 64 eps w / c(u1, u2)), since dh/du1 is the density c and near a corner at large theta it falls to about 5e-8 (amended 2026-11-15: the first text asked 1e-12 in u1 everywhere, which double precision cannot give at theta 2 and 4). | `tests/aggregation/test_clayton_sampling.py::test_h_function_inverse` |
| RV-1905 | THE `clayton_aggregate` SHALL return the VaR, ES and EC = VaR - EL of the row sums of Clayton-linked losses through `measures_from_sample`, as `copula_aggregate` does for the Gaussian and t families, approach the comonotonic sum as theta grows (theta = 200 within Monte Carlo error of `comonotonic_var`) and the independent aggregate as theta falls (theta = 0.01 within Monte Carlo error of the identity-correlation Gaussian aggregate), be reproducible for a seed, and reconcile its tau and copula with statsmodels' `ClaytonCopula` where the `stress` extra is installed. | `tests/aggregation/test_clayton_sampling.py::test_clayton_aggregate_limits_and_seed`, `tests/aggregation/test_clayton_sampling.py::test_reconciles_with_statsmodels` |
