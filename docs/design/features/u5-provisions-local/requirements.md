# U5-E2 local statutory provisions: CMF B-1 and Banco de Espana Annex 9

Sources: Contraste dossier 03 (read 2026-10-04): sections D.1 to D.6 for the CMF Compendio de Normas Contables para Bancos
(CNCB) Chapter B-1 (the structure, the individual-assessment categories, the default portfolio, the group assessment and
the max rule, the standard methods of Circular 2.346, additional provisions) and sections K.2 and K.3 for Banco de Espana
Circular 4/2017 Annex 9 (the categories, the coverage tables of points 144 and 140 as replaced by Circular 6/2021).
Contract: `engine-provisions-api` 1.0.0, modules `local_cl` and `local_es` (Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/provisions-api.md`).
Chilean loan provisions are B-1, never IFRS 9 impairment (dossier 03 D.1, dossier 02 F.5): no function here computes an
IFRS 9 ECL. The August 2026 CMF reform proposal (dossier 03 D.7) is not in force and is not implemented. The engine
computes; it states no compliance claim.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
Numbering starts at RV-550 (U5-E1 holds the lower range).
