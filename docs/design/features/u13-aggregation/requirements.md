# U13 Aggregation: the ladder from the simple sum to the worst-case bounds, the dependence band, model risk and the aggregation validation tests (U13-E1)

Sources: research dossier 05 of the Contraste initiative (market, CCR, ALM, liquidity, operational risk and stress; section G.1 the
aggregation ladder, G.2 Rosenberg and Schuermann, G.3 the dependence pitfalls and the band, G.4 the Euler allocation, G.5 the validation
tests), dossier 06 (B.12 model risk aggregation) and the U13 research addendum (F5, the rearrangement algorithm of Embrechts, Puccetti and
Ruschendorf 2013, with Tables 1 and 4 as golden values), and the aggregation API contract `engine-aggregation-api` 1.0.0 (Contraste
`docs/contracts/engine/aggregation-api.md`, Opus-reviewed with its fix round of 2026-10-19), whose functions, units, invariants and golden
values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/aggregation/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The engine computes
and states no compliance claim: the simple sum is the headline capital of the case and every other figure of the band is information, not
capital. Capital is economic capital on the unexpected-loss basis, `EC = VaR - EL` (dossier 04 l.652-653). The vine copula, Clayton
sampling and the BVAR are deferred (BL-078) and not written here. The golden values are reproduced by tests that recompute them through
a second, independent route, never by a test that only types the answer.
