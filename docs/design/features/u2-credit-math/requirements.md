# U2 credit risk mathematics: IRB, the large homogeneous portfolio, low-default estimation, PD curve calibration

Sources: research dossiers 01 (sections A and L), 03 (section C.2), 04 (sections C.1 to C.4) and 10 (the golden values,
read from the primary sources on 2026-10-05) of the Contraste initiative, transcribed into `docs/regulatory/` and
`docs/engines/`. Every regulatory parameter a function uses carries its paragraph reference and the in-force date of the
chapter it comes from.
