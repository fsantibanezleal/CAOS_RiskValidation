# U13 stress: satellites, scenario paths, the IAPE capital projection, reverse stress, scenario models and the stress validation tests (U13-E2)

Sources: dossier 05 of the Contraste initiative (section H, macro stress testing: H.1 the three layers, H.2 scenario generation, H.4 the
EBA 2025 test, H.5 reverse stress, H.7 what validators test), dossier 04 (I.1 and I.2, the satellites and the conditional PD), dossier 01
(J.4, the reverse stress formulation), dossier 03 (B.2 and B.4 the Annex 3 rows, G.4 the Chilean capital stack, H.2 the P2G proxy, M the
Santander Chile capital figures), the U13 research addendum (F6 the EBA note, F7 the Annex 3 form, F8 the NGFS variables) and the stress API
contract `engine-stress-api` 1.0.0 (Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/stress-api.md`, at commit ec6ca9e), whose functions, units, invariants and
golden values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/stress/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated. The engine computes and
states no compliance claim: the reverse stress statement and the P2G proxy are Contraste's, not regulatory text, and every light threshold is
a Contraste internal gate. Rates are decimals; money is one currency and never converted; a path keeps the unit of its source. The golden
values are reproduced by tests that recompute them through a second, independent route (a plain loop, `numpy.linalg.lstsq`, `scipy`, a closed
form), never by a test that only types the answer. No supervisory replication is built (decision E1: C19 stays planned).
