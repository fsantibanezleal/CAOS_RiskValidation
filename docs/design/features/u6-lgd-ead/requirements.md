# U6 LGD and EAD: the workout LGD, the LGD rungs, downturn, CCF and EAD, floors and the validation tests (U6-E)

Sources: research dossier 04 of the Contraste initiative (credit risk models, sections F.1 to F.4 for LGD and G.1 to G.3
for EAD and CCF) and dossier 01 (Basel pillar 1 and 2, section A.5 for the IRB LGD, EAD and CCF floors), and the LGD and EAD
API contract `engine-lgd-ead-api` 1.0.0 (Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/lgd-ead-api.md`, reviewed by Opus on 2026-10-13),
whose functions, units, invariants and golden values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/lgd_ead/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The engine computes and states no compliance claim: discount rates, downturn periods, thresholds and light parameters are
policy parameters the caller supplies. LGD and CCF values outside [0, 1] are kept and flagged, never silently clipped.
