# U10 IRRBB: curves, shocks, slotting, EVE and NII under SRP31 and RAN 21-13 Annex 1, behavioural models, simulation and the validation tests (U10-E)

Sources: research dossier 05 of the Contraste initiative (market, CCR, ALM, liquidity and operational risk; section D.1 to D.7 for
the IRRBB ladder, C.4 for Hull-White), dossier 01 (Basel pillar 1 and 2, section H.1 to H.5: SRP31.82 to SRP31.129, the shock
tables) and dossier 03 (Chile CMF and Spain BdE, section F.1 and F.2: RAN 21-13 Annex 1), and the IRRBB API contract
`engine-irrbb-api` (Contraste `docs/contracts/engine/irrbb-api.md`, reviewed by Opus on 2026-10-13), whose functions, units,
invariants and golden values this unit implements one by one. Transcribed into `docs/irrbb/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.
The engine computes and states no compliance claim. A positive delta EVE or delta NII is a loss. Discounting is continuously
compounded, `DF(t) = exp(-R t)`. A shock size outside the transcribed tables is refused unless the caller supplies it with its
label. The golden values are reproduced by tests that recompute them through a second, independent route, never by a test that
only types the answer.
