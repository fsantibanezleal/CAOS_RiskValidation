# U16-E3 packaged policies: one versioned policy file per Contraste battery (BL-085)

Sources: the unit sizing `plans/contraste/units/U16-sizing.md` (decisions D16-16 and D16-17), the 2,450 inline-string rows of the
committed Contraste case documents (read-only), and the light rules of the test functions of `riskvalidation.validation.*`.
Transcribed into `docs/policies.md`. Every threshold is a Contraste convention or internal gate, never a regulatory value.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| RV-1720 | THE engine SHALL ship one policy file per Contraste battery (aggregation, c12, ccr, ifrs9, irrbb, lgd-ead, liquidity, market, oprisk, portfolio, stress), named `policies/contraste-<name>-<version>.json` with the label `contraste-<name> <version>`, each entry stating a `convention:` source that names the producer cases and the dossier reference, AND `default.json` SHALL be unchanged. | `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_packaged_labels_list_the_default_and_the_eleven_batteries`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_every_packaged_file_loads_and_has_its_label`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_every_entry_states_its_convention_source_and_a_known_rule`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_default_json_changes_no_existing_threshold_and_keeps_its_label` |
| RV-1721 | THE `packaged_policy` SHALL load a packaged policy by its label and raise `KeyError` naming the label and the packaged labels for an absent one. | `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_packaged_policy_raises_keyerror_naming_the_label_and_the_packaged_labels`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_packaged_policy_returns_the_default_for_its_own_label` |
| RV-1722 | THE `Threshold` SHALL carry the optional frozen `parameters`, `rule`, `by` and `cases`, round-trip them through the JSON format, refuse non-finite parameters and a `by` without `cases` (or the reverse), AND leave the behavior of a threshold without them unchanged. | `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_parameters_round_trip_through_the_json_format`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_a_keyed_entry_needs_its_field_and_its_cases` |
| RV-1723 | THE `Policy.classify_row` SHALL pick the case of a keyed entry by the row's field and give `not_evaluated` for a test without an entry, a value without a case or a row without the field. | `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_a_keyed_entry_picks_the_case_by_the_row_field` |
| RV-1724 | THE `ccr.pit_exposure_backtest` entry SHALL hold no critical value and its rule SHALL read the row's `extras["simulated_critical_values"]`, AND an unknown rule SHALL raise `KeyError` naming the rules. | `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_the_pit_policy_holds_no_simulated_critical_value`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_an_unknown_rule_is_a_keyerror_naming_the_rules` |
| RV-1725 | THE packaged entry of every committed inline Contraste row SHALL hold every cut point of its old inline string (a keyed entry by the row's field) and the old per-case numbers SHALL equal the row's `extras`, AND the entry with its rule SHALL reproduce the committed light of each of the 2,450 rows. | `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_every_inline_string_maps_to_a_packaged_entry_with_its_cut_points`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_the_packaged_entry_reproduces_the_committed_light_of_every_inline_row` |
| RV-1726 | THE `resolve_policy` SHALL resolve a packaged battery label with source `packaged-policy` and the direction from the file, keep resolving every inline battery string (hyphenated families and `contraste-c12-1.0.0` included) and say so when a label has no file or no entry. | `tests/governance/test_governance_policy.py::test_a_packaged_battery_label_resolves_through_its_file_with_the_direction_from_the_file`, `tests/governance/test_governance_policy.py::test_a_packaged_battery_label_without_an_entry_or_a_file_says_so`, `tests/governance/test_governance_policy.py::test_inline_battery_strings_keep_resolving_including_the_hyphenated_and_c12_heads`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_the_inline_forms_keep_resolving_the_battery_heads_and_the_default_with_parameters` |
| RV-1728 | THE `policies/default.json` SHALL keep its label and change no existing threshold (it gains only `parameters`, no entry and no rule), AND the fairness and ml tests whose policy states no threshold SHALL stay without a policy row and be classified by their rule (`Policy.classify_row`). | `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_default_json_changes_no_existing_threshold_and_keeps_its_label`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_a_rule_only_test_is_classified_by_its_rule_whatever_the_policy_holds` |
| RV-1729 | THE `resolve_policy` SHALL resolve the packaged default label with parameters appended (`<label>; key=value; ...` and `<label>; ml-battery-1.1.0; ...`) as the bare label, reading the parameters and the battery, AND the fairness and ml test functions SHALL write the bare label, the 673 committed rows of that form and the 2,450 battery rows (3,123) being reproduced from their packaged entries. | `tests/governance/test_governance_policy.py::test_the_packaged_label_with_appended_parameters_resolves_as_the_bare_label`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_every_inline_string_maps_to_a_packaged_entry_with_its_cut_points`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_the_packaged_entry_reproduces_the_committed_light_of_every_inline_row`, `tests/test_packaged_policies.py::test_the_inline_forms_keep_resolving_the_battery_heads_and_the_default_with_parameters` |
