# U4 rating transitions

Sources: research dossier 13 of the Contraste initiative (read from the primary sources on 2026-10-06: Israel,
Rosenthal and Wei 2001 for the embedding problem and its three published matrices; Anderson and Goodman 1957 for the
estimators and the time-homogeneity, order and specified-probability tests; Schuermann and Hanson 2004 (FRBNY Staff
Report 190) for the cohort and duration estimators, the industry treatment of withdrawals and the intervals for a PD
by grade; Smith and dos Reis 2018 for the EM generator of snapshot data with its closed-form E-step; dos Reis,
Pfeuffer and Smith 2020 for rating momentum, its hazard test and its self-exciting model; Engelmann 2024 for the
through-the-cycle portfolio a migration matrix implies; ESMA's CEREP help file for the stock model of published
matrices; EBA/GL/2017/16 for the one-year default rate and the long-run average). Transcribed into `docs/transitions/`.

EARS (Mavin et al., RE'09, doi:10.1109/RE.2009.9). Every requirement names the gate that fails when it is violated.

| ID | Requirement | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| RV-401 | THE cohort estimator SHALL return, from a count matrix with or without a withdrawals column, the transition matrix, the row sizes and the counts, either removing withdrawals and renormalising each row (the industry treatment Schuermann and Hanson describe) or keeping them as an absorbing state; pooled over periods it SHALL be Anderson and Goodman's (2.8), per period their (2.9); and it SHALL refuse negative counts and rows of different lengths. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_cohort_estimator` |
| RV-402 | THE duration estimator SHALL return from obligor paths the maximum likelihood generator q_ij = K_ij / S_i of the fully observed chain, and THE Aalen-Johansen estimator the product-integral matrix between two times; on paths drawn from a known generator each SHALL recover the truth within four Monte Carlo standard errors, and the cohort estimator SHALL put zero on cells the truth makes positive where no path made that move in a year. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_duration_and_aalen_johansen_recover_truth` |
| RV-403 | THE embedding module SHALL compute the log series of Israel, Rosenthal and Wei with its convergence condition S < 1, report which of the three non-existence conditions of their Theorem 3 hold, return the diagonal (2), weighted (2') and JLT (3) generators with the L1 distance of exp(Q) to P, check stochastic monotonicity (their Lemma 1), and reproduce their nine published distances to the printed six digits and their first matrix's series generator to six decimals. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_embedding_conditions_and_adjustments`, `tests/test_reproductions.py::test_israel_rosenthal_wei` |
| RV-404 | THE EM generator SHALL estimate a generator from count matrices observed at fixed steps by the algorithm of Bladt and Sorensen with the closed-form expectations of Smith and dos Reis (Van Loan block exponentials), its log-likelihood SHALL not decrease from one iteration to the next, its expected jump counts and holding times SHALL agree with a simulation of the conditioned paths, and on counts drawn from a known generator it SHALL recover the truth within the tolerance the harness measures. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_em_generator` |
| RV-405 | THE time-homogeneity test SHALL test constant transition probabilities across T periods by Anderson and Goodman's likelihood ratio (3.5) and chi-square (3.8), with (T - 1) m(m - 1) degrees of freedom less the cells with no expectation, and SHALL hold its size on a homogeneous chain within the harness's bound. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_time_homogeneity`, `tests/test_size.py::test_size_of_every_p_value_test` |
| RV-406 | THE order test SHALL test a first-order chain against a second-order one from triplet counts by Anderson and Goodman's (3.12), m(m - 1)^2 degrees of freedom less the empty cells, and SHALL hold its size on a Markov chain. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_markov_order`, `tests/test_size.py::test_size_of_every_p_value_test` |
| RV-407 | THE specified-probability test SHALL test a count matrix against a reference transition matrix by Anderson and Goodman's (3.1), summed over rows. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_matrix_against_reference` |
| RV-408 | THE momentum test SHALL test, from obligor paths, whether being downgraded into the current grade raises the intensity of leaving it (the semi-parametric hazard lambda(t) = q_i(t) exp(c Z(t)) by partial likelihood, the Markov null c = 0), for downgrades and upgrades separately, and SHALL hold its size on a Markov chain. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_momentum_hazard`, `tests/test_size.py::test_size_of_every_p_value_test` |
| RV-409 | THE interval for a PD by grade SHALL offer the Wald interval on firm-years (Schuermann and Hanson's (2.2)), the Agresti-Coull interval (their (3.3)) with the dependence correction of their (3.4), the Jeffreys interval, and the bootstrap of the duration PD by resampling histories (their section 2.3) and parametric (Christensen, Hansen and Lando), each refusing an empty grade, and SHALL reproduce their Table 5's analytical intervals to the printed digits. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_pd_intervals`, `tests/test_reproductions.py::test_schuermann_hanson_table5` |
| RV-410 | THE mobility metrics SHALL return Jafry and Schuermann's mean singular value of P - I and the trace index (n - tr P)/(n - 1), both zero on the identity. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_mobility` |
| RV-411 | THE through-the-cycle portfolio SHALL be the unique solution of Engelmann's (10) for a matrix whose performing block is primitive and an origination with nothing in default, computed by iterating his propagation (9) from any start, with the projected default-rate path; it SHALL refuse a matrix or origination outside Theorem 1's conditions, and SHALL reproduce his section 4 (the TTC portfolio of the matrix (11) and the extremes of its projections) to the printed digits. | `tests/test_transitions.py::test_ttc_portfolio`, `tests/test_reproductions.py::test_engelmann_ttc_portfolio` |
| RV-412 | THE rating-path generator SHALL draw exact continuous-time paths from a generator (competing exponential clocks), optionally with a self-exciting downgrade intensity (dos Reis et al.'s model), a regime switch of the generator at given calendar times, and withdrawals (non-informative, or rising before default), and SHALL give snapshots at given times in CEREP's stock model; its truth SHALL be exact (the generator, exp(tQ), the PD by grade at each horizon), and its verifier SHALL reproduce the jump counts and holding times of the Markov case within four Monte Carlo standard errors. | `tests/test_generators.py::test_rating_paths` |
| RV-413 | EVERY new test SHALL be registered with its reference and appear in the size study of `tests/test_size.py` at its null. | `tests/test_size.py::test_size_of_every_p_value_test` |
| RV-414 | THE documentation SHALL hold one page per part (estimators, the embedding problem, Markov tests, intervals and mobility, the TTC portfolio, the generator) with their equations, sources and the published reproduction, and the docs index and README SHALL link them. | `tests/test_docs.py` |
