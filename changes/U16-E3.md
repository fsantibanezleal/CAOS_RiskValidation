- policies: one packaged, versioned policy file per Contraste battery (`contraste-aggregation-battery`, `contraste-c12`, `contraste-ccr-battery`, `contraste-ifrs9-battery`, `contraste-irrbb-battery`, `contraste-lgd-ead-battery`, `contraste-liquidity-battery`, `contraste-market-battery`, `contraste-oprisk-battery`, `contraste-portfolio-battery`, `contraste-stress-battery`, version 1.0.0), 79 entries, every source a `convention:` (BL-085, decisions D16-16 and D16-17); documented in `docs/policies.md`.
- core.policy: `Threshold` gains the optional `parameters`, `rule`, `by` and `cases`; new public names `packaged_policy(label)`, `packaged_labels()` and `Policy.classify_row`, plus `Threshold.for_row` and `Threshold.classify_row`. The integrator adds `packaged_policy` and `packaged_labels` to `riskvalidation.core.__all__` at the merge. `default.json`, `default_policy` and `load_policy` are unchanged.
- policies.rules: the classifiers a `rule` names (`classify_rule`, `RULES`); the light of each of the 2,450 committed Contraste inline rows is reproduced from its packaged entry.
- validation: every `_version` call site of the ten battery modules writes the packaged label (`contraste-<name> <version>`) and the parameters left the string; the seeds, `rho`, `min_months`, the Gauss-Hermite nodes, the `near` and `far` days, `coverage` and `window` moved to `extras`; the lifetime-PD back-test writes `predicted`, `observed` and `rho`; `euler_additivity` gains `basis`, written to `extras["basis"]`. No light rule changed.
- fairness and ml: the `fair.*` and `ml.*` test functions write the bare label `riskvalidation-default 2026.10.1` (673 committed rows carried `; key=value; ...` appended); the parameters are in `extras["thresholds"]`, the ml battery version in `extras["battery"]`. `policies/default.json` keeps its label and every threshold and gains only `parameters` on 12 entries (no entry, no rule); `Policy.classify_row` classifies the contract's no-row tests (`rules.RULE_ONLY`) by their rule.
- governance.policy: the default label with parameters appended resolves as the bare label (parameters and battery read); a packaged battery label resolves with source `packaged-policy` and the direction from the file (null with its reason for a p-value or a compound rule); the inline battery form keeps resolving and now also matches hyphenated families (`lgd-ead`) and `contraste-c12-1.0.0`.
